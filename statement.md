# Project Statement: Personal Finance & Budget Analyzer

## Problem Statement
Many individuals struggle to track their monthly spending and understand how their money is allocated across essential needs versus discretionary wants. Without clear insights into their savings rates and spending habits, users often overspend on non-essential items and miss standard financial targets.

## Scope & Target Users
- **Scope:** A lightweight terminal-based personal budgeting tool that calculates savings metrics, identifies the largest expense, evaluates budget health, and gives automated financial recommendations.
- **Target Users:** Students, young professionals, and anyone seeking a quick, simple way to evaluate their monthly budget without complex spreadsheets or paid software.

## High-Level Features
1. **Interactive Console Input:** Captures income and detailed expense breakdown.
2. **Needs vs. Wants Analysis:** Groups expenses to track compliance with standard budgeting rules.
3. **Automated Insights & Alerts:** Generates conditional warnings when spending in specific categories exceeds ideal percentage thresholds.
4. **Target Tracking:** Compares current savings against a standard 20% savings recommendation.
